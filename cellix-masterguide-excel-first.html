01 — Market Landscape

The AI Spreadsheet Battlefield

When Anthropic launched Claude in Excel in 2025, a lot of founders panicked. They shouldn't have. Claude's move validates the market — it doesn't close it. Here is the honest picture of what actually happened and where the gaps are.

The Core Truth

Claude in Excel is a horizontal feature bundled with a $20/month English-language subscription, built for US/EU finance professionals who already use Claude. It does not speak GST. It does not understand Tally exports. It does not have an ICAI-grade audit trail. It does not price for ₹999/month. The Indian CA in Coimbatore filing GSTR-2B from a messy Tally dump is simply not Anthropic's user. That CA is CELLIX's user. You are not competing with Anthropic. You are filling the market they didn't build for.

Why Claude's Entry is Good News for You

Anthropic just spent millions educating the world that AI inside spreadsheets is real, useful, and worth paying for. They validated the product category. Indian CAs will now hear about "AI for Excel/Sheets" from multiple directions — Claude, Gemini, news coverage. When they search for an India-specific alternative that handles GST and is priced in rupees, CELLIX is the answer. Anthropic is doing your top-of-funnel for free.

Market Timing Signal

Indian CAs, audit firms, and enterprise finance teams overwhelmingly use Microsoft Excel — it is the professional standard for client files, audit working papers, and statutory schedules. The accounting services market is growing at 10.17% CAGR to 2033. AI startup funding hit ₹6,500 crore in 2024 — a 40% year-on-year increase. Starting with Excel puts CELLIX directly inside the environment India's most valuable finance professionals already trust.

06 — Segment Winners

Who Wins Which User Segment

The market is not winner-takes-all. Different products win different segments. Here is the honest breakdown of who dominates where — and why CELLIX owns the segments that matter for India.

🇮🇳 Indian CAs — Solo Practices (72K firms)

CELLIX Winsby wide margin

Zero competition here. No global tool handles GST recon, Tally cleanup, or ICAI audit trails. No local tool has AI-powered spreadsheet manipulation. This segment is CELLIX's to lose. Win condition: solve GST reconciliation reliably, price at ₹999/month, and get 3 CA WhatsApp groups spreading the word.

CELLIX Advantage

Every single differentiator you have maps directly to this segment's pain. This is your beachhead.

🇮🇳 CA Firms — 2–10 Staff (28K+ firms)

CELLIX Winswith team plan

The "quality gate on junior staff work" positioning is extremely powerful for this segment. Senior partners want to review junior spreadsheet work before it reaches clients. CELLIX's mandatory approval workflow and audit trail are literally what these partners need. Team plan at ₹6,999/month covers 5 seats — about ₹1,400/person, well within budget for firms billing clients at ₹1,500–5,000/hour.

🇮🇳 Indian SME Finance Teams (7.7M digitally mature SMEs)

CELLIX WinsPhase 2 target

After establishing in Excel (Phase 1), CELLIX's Phase 2 Google Sheets add-on directly serves the SME stack. Tally + Sheets is the standard for Indian SME finance — and CAs who adopt CELLIX on Excel will recommend it to their SME clients when the Sheets version launches. MIS reports, cash flow dashboards, vendor reconciliation — all recurring monthly workflows that create strong retention. Win this segment with use-case-specific templates for each industry vertical (trading, manufacturing, retail).

🇺🇸 US/EU Enterprise Excel Users

Shortcut Winsby a mile

Macro compatibility, 100% Excel parity, SOC 2, zero-retention AI deals, RBAC — all specifically designed for US/EU financial institutions. This is not CELLIX's market and you should not try to compete here. Shortcut has a multi-year head start in product depth and enterprise trust for this specific segment.

🌐 M365 Orgs — Decks + Docs + Excel

o11 Winsbreadth advantage

For organizations needing AI across PowerPoint, Word, data rooms, and Excel with unlimited context and citations — o11's full M365 suite is hard to beat. CELLIX doesn't compete here (and shouldn't try to). If an Indian GCC wants full M365 AI, o11 is a real option. Your angle in GCCs is the finance team specifically, not the whole org.

💻 Individual Claude Subscribers (Global)

Claude Winsfree marginal cost

If someone already pays ₹1,700/month for Claude Pro, the Excel add-in is free on top. For general formula debugging and model Q&A, this is compelling enough. CELLIX should not try to win this segment. Your users are India-specific professionals who need GST, Tally, and compliance — not general-purpose LLM subscribers.

The Global Race Result

There is no single winner. Shortcut owns deep Excel governance for US/EU finance. o11 owns M365 breadth for cross-app AI. Claude in Excel is the default for existing Claude subscribers. And India — CAs, SMEs, startups, and enterprises — is an unclaimed market. No one is building for the CA filing GSTR-2B from a Tally export at ₹999/month with an ICAI audit trail. That is your market. You don't compete in the global race — you run a completely different race on a completely different track.

Apps Script is Google's official, commercially supported add-on platform with thousands of enterprise deployments. Major restrictions are very unlikely without long deprecation notice. Additionally, building toward the Excel add-in (Phase 2) means you're not single-platform dependent by Month 6. The shared backend architecture means a platform switch doesn't require rebuilding your AI layer.
AI hallucinations on GST rules damage trust
If CELLIX makes an incorrect GST classification and a CA doesn't catch it, it could damage client relationships and CELLIX's reputation.
This is why the verifier layer, mandatory preview, and risk scoring exist. CELLIX should never silently apply any GST-related formula or classification. Every suggestion shows confidence score and risk level. The mandatory approval workflow means a CA always sees and approves before anything touches their file. Additionally, encode GST rules as hard constraints (not LLM prompts) where possible — rules-based logic for known GST rates, formula patterns, and eligibility conditions reduces hallucination risk fundamentally.
LLM API costs erode margins as scale increases
At ₹2.5–3 per API call, high-volume users on the free or low-tier plan could make unit economics negative.
The credit system directly ties API cost to credit consumption. Limit free tier to 50 credits/month (~₹125–150 in API costs at most). Pro tier at ₹999/month with 500 credits = ~₹1,250–1,500 in API costs = 60%+ gross margin before hosting/infra. As you scale, negotiate volume discounts with OpenAI/Anthropic (typical at $1M+/year API spend). Additionally, cache frequent GST rule queries and template generations to reduce repeat API calls — these are deterministic, not user-specific.
