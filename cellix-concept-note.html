What is CELLIX

Intelligent Excel AI built for Indian CAs and finance professionals

CELLIX is a tool that works as a sidebar inside Google Sheets and Microsoft Excel. You do not switch to another app or website. You do not copy-paste your data anywhere. You simply open a side panel within your existing spreadsheet, describe what you need in plain language, and CELLIX suggests what to do — one step at a time, with your full review and approval before anything changes.

Think of it as having an experienced assistant sitting next to you while you work. That assistant can read your entire spreadsheet, understand its structure, suggest the right formulas, flag errors, and help you complete tasks that currently take hours — without ever touching a single cell without your permission.

The Problem We Are Solving

The spreadsheet work that consumes CA practices today

Every practicing CA and finance professional in India knows how much time disappears into spreadsheet tasks every month. Not the thinking work — the mechanical, repetitive, error-prone work that does not require professional judgment but still consumes professional time.

  • GST Reconciliation — GSTR-2A / 2B vs Purchase Register Matching hundreds or thousands of rows from the GSTN portal against your purchase register. Supplier invoice numbers that don't match exactly. Amounts split across two entries. Credit notes with negative values. Date format differences. This work takes 3 to 6 hours per client every single month — and a small mistake means a missed ITC claim.
  • Tally Export Cleanup When you export a ledger from Tally, what you get is rarely clean. The rupee symbol sits inside numeric columns. Dates come in three different formats in the same file. Merged cells break any formula you try to apply. Blank rows are scattered throughout. Cleaning this before you can use the data takes hours of junior staff time every week.
  • Audit Working Papers and Documentation After every change made to a client file, there should be a record of what changed, when, and who approved it. In practice, this documentation is either done manually, done incompletely, or not done at all. Reconstruction during an ICAI review is painful.
  • ITC Computation and Verification Calculating eligible Input Tax Credit, applying reversal rules, and cross-checking with GSTR-2B data requires understanding both the law and the spreadsheet structure simultaneously. One wrong column reference or one missed rule creates a cascading error.
  • Ind-AS and Schedule III Preparation Mapping a trial balance to a Schedule III balance sheet or an Ind-AS compliant P&L involves knowing exactly which line item belongs where — and then building the spreadsheet structure and formulas to pull everything together correctly.
  • Client MIS Reports Monthly management information reports — P&L summaries, cash flow statements, budget vs actual dashboards — are often rebuilt from scratch or heavily reformatted every month even though the structure rarely changes.

None of these tasks require a bad CA to make an error. A good CA with 10 years of experience still spends the same hours on these mechanical tasks as a first-year articled assistant. The difference is that the senior CA's time is worth far more — and is being consumed by work that should not require their expertise at all.

How It Works

Simple in experience, careful in execution

CELLIX is designed around one principle: nothing changes in your spreadsheet unless you explicitly approve it. Here is what a typical interaction looks like, from start to finish.

1

You open the CELLIX sidebar inside your Sheets or Excel file

There is no separate app to open. A small panel appears on the right side of your spreadsheet — the same way any other add-on panel appears. Your data stays exactly where it is. Nothing is sent anywhere automatically.

2

You describe what you need in plain language

You type something like: "Reconcile my purchase register against my GSTR-2A data and show me the mismatches" — or — "My Tally export has rupee symbols in the amount columns, fix them and standardize the date format" — or — "Create a GSTR-3B summary from this data." You do not need to know any formula or technical command.

3

CELLIX reads your spreadsheet and prepares a plan

CELLIX looks at your sheet — the column headers, the data structure, the existing formulas — and creates a step-by-step plan for what it proposes to do. It does not start making changes yet. It shows you the plan first, in plain language that you can read and evaluate.

4

You see a preview of every proposed change before anything is applied

For each proposed change, CELLIX shows you: which cells or columns will be affected, what the current value or formula is, what the new formula or value will be, and why it is suggesting this change. It also tells you if any suggestion carries a risk — if it is confident, it says so; if something is uncertain, it flags it.

5

You approve or reject each suggestion, one at a time

Nothing is applied automatically. You click Approve for what you want and Reject for what you do not. You can approve all, reject all, or review each one individually. Your professional judgment stays in control throughout. CELLIX never overrides your decision.

6

Every approved change is logged automatically in an audit trail

Once you approve a change and it is applied to the spreadsheet, CELLIX records it: what changed, which cell or range, what the formula was, when it was applied, and who approved it. This log is always available and can be exported as a document — useful for working paper documentation, internal review, or ICAI purposes.

7

You can undo any change at any point

Even after a change has been applied, it can be reversed. Not just the last action — any action from the session. CELLIX maintains a complete record of everything it has done, so you are never locked into a change you later want to revisit.

What CELLIX Is Designed to Handle

Workflows built specifically for Indian accounting practice

CELLIX is not a general-purpose tool that has been adapted for India. It is being designed from the ground up around the actual workflows that Indian CAs and finance professionals deal with every day.

🧾

GST Reconciliation

GSTR-2A and GSTR-2B matching against your purchase register. Handles split invoices, credit notes, date mismatches, slight amount differences, and missing entries. Shows you matched, partially matched, and unmatched rows with reasons for each mismatch. Generates a reconciliation statement ready for client files.

📁

Tally Export Cleanup

Automatically detects and fixes the common problems in Tally ledger exports: rupee symbols embedded in amount fields, inconsistent date formats within the same file, merged cells that break formulas, blank rows, duplicate entries, and column headers that don't match standard naming. Turns a raw Tally export into a clean, formula-ready spreadsheet without you touching each cell manually.

✅

Audit Trail and Working Paper Log

Every change made through CELLIX — every formula written, every column cleaned, every reconciliation applied — is logged automatically with the date, time, the name of the person who approved it, and exactly what changed. This log can be exported as a PDF document formatted as a working paper. No more manual documentation of spreadsheet work.

📊

Formula Generation and Verification

You describe what you want the formula to do in plain language and CELLIX writes it. Before showing you the formula, it tests it against the actual data in your sheet to verify the output is what you expected. It also checks for common mistakes — wrong column references, mismatched ranges, circular references — before any formula is proposed to you.

📋

ITC Computation Templates

Prebuilt templates for Input Tax Credit calculations, including the eligibility split between blocked and eligible credit, reversal computations under Rule 42 and Rule 43, and reconciliation with GSTR-2B data. The rules are encoded into the tool — you supply the data, CELLIX applies the correct logic and shows you how each figure was arrived at.

📑

Ind-AS Schedule Generation

Provide your trial balance data and CELLIX maps each ledger head to the correct Schedule III classification, generates the balance sheet and profit and loss structure, and builds the formulas that pull everything together. Handles the most common classification decisions — current vs non-current, operating vs non-operating — and flags items where human judgment is needed.

🔍

Error Detection and Audit Scan

CELLIX scans your spreadsheet for the most common problems in accounting files: broken formula references, division by zero errors, inconsistent ranges, merged cells that are hiding data, columns where numbers and text are mixed, and date fields that are stored as text. For each issue it finds, it explains what the problem is and suggests how to fix it — without making any change until you approve.

📈

MIS Report Templates

Reusable monthly report templates for P&L summaries, cash flow statements, and budget vs actual comparisons. Once set up for a client, the same template can be refreshed every month by simply updating the source data — the structure, formulas, and formatting carry over automatically.

🏦

Bank Reconciliation

Match your cash book or ledger entries against your bank statement, handling the usual complications — UPI transactions with partial reference matches, timing differences, bounced instruments, and bank charges that appear in the statement but not yet in the books. Produces a clear reconciliation statement showing matched and unmatched entries with reasons.

📬

26AS / TDS Matching

Cross-check your TDS ledger entries against the data in Form 26AS. Flags deductor errors, timing differences between deduction and deposit, and entries that appear in 26AS but are missing from the books — before you discover them at the time of ITR filing.

Important Boundaries

What CELLIX is designed never to do

Understanding the boundaries of the product is as important as understanding what it does. These are deliberate design decisions, not limitations.

CELLIX will never apply any change to your spreadsheet without your explicit approval. There is no auto-apply, no background editing, no silent modification. Every proposed change must be reviewed and accepted by you before it touches a single cell.

CELLIX does not give tax advice or legal opinions. It applies rules and computations based on the information you provide and the parameters encoded in the tool. Any judgment about how a specific client situation should be treated — whether a particular expense is deductible, whether a particular supply is taxable — remains with you, the professional.

CELLIX does not store your client data. The information in your spreadsheet is used only to complete the task you have requested in that session. Nothing is retained or stored on external servers once the session is complete. Enterprise users who need audit log retention can choose that option separately.

CELLIX does not replace the CA. It removes the mechanical burden from the CA's work. The professional judgment, the client relationship, the sign-off on every output — that remains entirely with you.

Why India, Why Now

Built for the reality of Indian accounting practice, not adapted from elsewhere

Most productivity tools for spreadsheets are built for a Western financial context. They assume clean data, standard column headers, and workflows that do not involve GST, Tally, the ICAI, or the specific compliance requirements of Indian law. When Indian CAs try to use these tools, they find themselves spending time adapting the tool to their context rather than using it to do their work.

CELLIX starts from the other end. The workflows it handles — GST reconciliation, Tally cleanup, ITC computation, Ind-AS preparation — are the core of Indian CA practice. The edge cases it is built to handle — split invoices, credit notes, Tally date formats, blocked credit categories — are the edge cases that actually appear in Indian client files, not theoretical examples from a textbook.

India has more than 400,000 practicing Chartered Accountants. The vast majority of them use spreadsheets for a significant portion of their client work. There is currently no tool that treats their specific workflows as the primary use case. That is the gap CELLIX is being built to fill.
